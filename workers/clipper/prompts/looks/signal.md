## Look for this clip: Signal Grid (dark systems)

This replaces the dark default above. The full template is in
docs/style_guide_signal_grid.md; the parts that matter here:

- World `#181818` with a 1px `#242424` grid (cells ~154px) and a `#4A4A4A`
  dot at each cell centre, on every moment.
- Processes are chains of colour-coded step blocks: each kind of step keeps
  one colour for the whole clip (blue `#4A86DC`, orange `#E68C5F`, green
  `#2AAA6A`, yellow `#F0DC0A` joints, purple `#A082F0`, pink `#F0A0D2`). Blocks
  have square corners and a gentle left-to-right gradient. "Everything else"
  is grey `#5A5A5A`; the one thing that matters is the brand accent.
- One monospace for all text (use "Inter Tight" if no mono is loaded), regular
  weight, lowercase-friendly.
- Motion is camera-like: follow the chain, push in 2-3x to read a detail,
  pull back until the scene is one cell of the grid ("one of many"). Labels
  are revealed by their block growing; text lines appear one after another;
  things leave by being left behind, not by fading in place.
- Halftone grey clouds are allowed as the one organic texture, sparingly.
